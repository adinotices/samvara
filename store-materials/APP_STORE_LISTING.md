### Full Description
(max 4000 chars)

Saṃvara is an accountability app that puts you in control. Make commitments, track metrics, and face real consequences (charges) only when you fail.

**How It Works**

1. **Make a Commitment** — Set a goal (e.g., "No porn") with a time frame (7 days) and stake ($5).
2. **Track It** — Every day, confirm you kept your commitment clean.
3. **Handle Lapses** — If you slip or miss, you're charged immediately. No hiding. No excuses.
4. **Move Forward** — After a charge, recommit at a higher stake and try again.

**Why Saṃvara Works**

- **Real Consequences** — Charges actually hit your wallet, making accountability real.
- **Flexible Recommitment** — Increase your stake to rebuild trust. You're always in control.
- **Multi-Metric Tracking** — Track as many habits as you need (porn, caffeine, exercise, etc.).
- **Secure & Private** — Your data is encrypted, stored in the US, and never sold to advertisers.
- **No Judgment** — We don't tell you what to commit to—you do. Saṃvara just enforces your own rules.

**Key Features**

- **Deadline Notifications** — Get reminded before your commitment expires.
- **Metric Dashboard** — See your progress across all your commitments.
- **Device Tracking** — Know where you're signed in; revoke sessions remotely.
- **Export Your Data** — Download all your data in portable JSON format (GDPR-compliant).
- **Session Management** — Sign out of specific devices or everywhere at once.
- **Audit Logs** — See a complete record of your activity for compliance.

**Privacy First**

- No analytics SDKs. No tracking pixels. No data sold to advertisers.
- All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3+).
- Session tokens hashed, never stored in plaintext.
- App-private storage on your device; other apps can't access your data.
- GDPR & CCPA compliant. Export or delete your data anytime.

**Real Charges, Securely Processed**

Saṃvara charges your card directly — through Stripe, a trusted payment processor used by millions of apps — when you slip or miss a commitment. There's no separate account to create, no middleman: you add a card once, and Saṃvara handles the rest. Charges are real; your accountability is too.

**Perfect For**

- Breaking porn/sexual content addictions
- Cutting sugar or caffeine
- Building exercise habits
- Overcoming procrastination
- Staying accountable to your goals

**One More Thing**

Saṃvara is young. We're actively building new features based on user feedback. Have an idea? Email us at support@samvara.app.

**Download Saṃvara today and start building the habits you actually want.**

### Review Expectations

**Google Play:**
- Typically approves within 2-4 hours
- May reject for: privacy policy issues, unclear billing, content violations
- May request clarification on "real charges" (explain: direct Stripe billing tied to a commitment the user set themselves, not gambling)

**Apple App Store:**
- Typically approves within 24-48 hours
- More strict on privacy; ensure Privacy Policy is clear
- May ask about real charges (explain: direct Stripe billing, user consent, card added explicitly by the user)
- Check "Requires explanation" box if needed
